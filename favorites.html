Mindfulness

Waking Up

Sam Harris's app has guided meditations, lessons, and conversations about the mind. It's a good place to practice paying attention, rather than just read about it.

If the price is a barrier, Waking Up offers free membership through its scholarship program. No explanation required.

Explore Waking Up ↗

Place · Indianapolis

North Mass Boulder

Climbing, training, and a café in Indianapolis. It turns a workout into a series of little puzzles, with people to hang out with between attempts.

Visit North Mass Boulder ↗

Writing & web

Derek Sivers & his website

Essays, book notes, and a “now” page from Derek Sivers. His writing is direct and curious, and his site feels like a person made it.

Read Derek Sivers ↗

Music software

SongDrive

mickmister's project for writing and collaborating on songs. It gives half-formed musical ideas somewhere to live while people work on them together.

Explore SongDrive ↗

Open knowledge

Wikipedia

The free encyclopedia that can turn one question into an entire afternoon. It's a rabbit hole with citations.

Explore Wikipedia ↗

Digital freedom

Free Software Foundation

A nonprofit that defends the freedom to use, study, share, and change software. That freedom is easy to take for granted until a tool you rely on won't let you do something basic.

Meet the FSF ↗

Creative experiment

Oasis

Decart's Minecraft-like experiment draws each new frame as you play. The glitches are part of the fascination: you can see how far the idea has come and where it still falls apart.

Try Oasis ↗

Learning tool

Anki

Flashcards with review timing that adapts to what you remember. Small study sessions add up, without the last-minute cram.

Explore Anki ↗

Free software

GNU

A free operating system project whose tools are used all over computing. GNU insists that users should be able to inspect and change the software they run.

Explore GNU ↗

The open web

A JavaScript environment in every browser

Every browser ships with a JavaScript console. Open it and you can test an idea immediately, even on a computer with nothing installed.

Learn how to use the console ↗

Course · Free access

Learning How to Learn

Barbara Oakley and Terrence Sejnowski explain memory, focus, and procrastination in useful terms. It makes studying feel less mysterious.

Oakley shared a free link to the full course; that version doesn't include a certificate.

Take the free course ↗

The weird web

The Crawler Zoo

A live menagerie of bots visiting one website, complete with exhibits and traps for crawlers that ignore robots.txt. Watching the visitor log is surprisingly entertaining.

Enter The Crawler Zoo ↗

YouTube · Outdoors

Camping With Steve

Steve Wallis films camping trips without much polish or fuss. I come for the odd camping spots and stay for Steve's low-key, practical narration.

Watch Camping With Steve ↗

Essay

High Agency

George Mack's long essay is about finding a way through obstacles instead of waiting for instructions. I come back to it when I'm waiting for a perfect plan instead of trying something.

Read High Agency ↗

Game & music

Undertale

Toby Fox's RPG is funny, strange, and unexpectedly tender. Its soundtrack does so much of the storytelling that a few notes can put me right back in a scene.

Explore Undertale ↗

Math video

3Blue1Brown on Fourier transforms

A mixed signal can hold several frequencies at once, and a Fourier transform can tease them apart. 3Blue1Brown makes that feel as startling as un-mixing swirled paint.

Watch the Fourier transform video ↗

Science video

Which Way Is Down?

In about half an hour, Vsauce shows how straight paths on a curved surface can meet. Freely falling objects that start at rest still move through time, and their straightest paths through curved spacetime can converge. Gravity as geometry still feels like magic.

Watch Vsauce's explanation ↗

The natural world

The boreal forest

A forest that wraps around the top of the world, with spruce, bogs, lakes, and a short but busy summer. I like that its quiet is never really empty.

Explore the boreal forest ↗

Whiskers on Kittens

Tiny face, enormous whiskers. This list got better the moment the kitten showed up.
