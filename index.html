What drives me

I am a man of three quests:

Code

I love the internet, and I am passionate about building software on it for and with anyone who is interested. I love making people's lives better, more fun, and less painful. I love innovative and clever technologies, and I also love beautiful and intuitive user interfaces. I also love music software and am working on software that interfaces with DAWs, like file-management systems, a music platform for musicians, and novel convolution-based audio plugins.

Explore my work →

Music

Music is basically part of the fundamental architecture of my brain. I love to improvise it with anyone who is interested. I love to produce it. I love owning every aspect of the production of a song. I love songwriting. I love folk music like Fleet Foxes, Joni Mitchell, and Nick Drake, and I am enamored with my acoustic guitar. I love electronic music like Pretty Lights, Porter Robinson, and HOME. I love the fine details of sound design and interesting textures in music. I love rock music—everything from the Beatles to Causa Sui, Khruangbin, Crumb, Tame Impala, and the Arctic Monkeys. I want to create a fusion genre that is folk, rock, and electronic; that blends composed and improvised music; and that is danceable while still deeply introspective and personal. I also think things like genre and scale—really, any constraint—are optional. I am very open to collaborating with anyone who is interested.

Work

Useful software with a human reason behind it.

I have built tools for real workflows, small business websites, playful creative interfaces, and music-centered systems that turn theory into something a person can actually use.

Some of that work is about clarity: making a local business easier to understand, making an interface feel less scattered, or helping someone get to the next useful action. Some of it is about curiosity: building tools that understand chords, MIDI settings, and harmonic relationships, with little moments of personality that make software feel alive.
