About me

I am a man of three quests:

Code

I’ve been coding since high school. I love the internet, and I like building software that makes people’s lives a little easier or a little more fun. I’ve written software for international fashion brands and for local IT, music tech, and real estate businesses.

I’m also working on music software: file management for DAWs, a platform for musicians, and convolution-based audio plugins.

See my work →

Music

Music is basically part of the fundamental architecture of my brain. I love to improvise it with anyone who is interested. I love to produce it. I love handling every part of a song’s production myself. I love songwriting.

I love folk music like Fleet Foxes, Joni Mitchell, and Nick Drake, and I am enamored with my acoustic guitar. I love electronic music like Pretty Lights, Porter Robinson, and HOME. I love the fine details of sound design and interesting textures in music. I love rock music, from the Beatles and the Arctic Monkeys to Tame Impala, Khruangbin, Crumb, and Causa Sui.

I want to make a fusion of folk, rock, and electronic music that mixes composed parts with improvisation. Something you can dance to that still feels introspective and personal. I also think genre and scale, and really any constraint, are optional.
