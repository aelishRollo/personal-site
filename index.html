What drives me

I am a man of three quests:

Code

I’ve been coding since high school. I love the internet and building software that makes people’s lives better, more fun, and less painful. I’ve written software for local IT, music technology, and real estate businesses, and for international fashion brands.

I’m also working on music software: file management for DAWs, a platform for musicians, and convolution-based audio plugins.

Explore my work →

Music

Music is basically part of the fundamental architecture of my brain. I love to improvise it with anyone who is interested. I love to produce it. I love owning every aspect of the production of a song. I love songwriting.

I love folk music like Fleet Foxes, Joni Mitchell, and Nick Drake, and I am enamored with my acoustic guitar. I love electronic music like Pretty Lights, Porter Robinson, and HOME. I love the fine details of sound design and interesting textures in music. I love rock music—everything from the Beatles to Causa Sui, Khruangbin, Crumb, Tame Impala, and the Arctic Monkeys.

I want to create a fusion genre that is folk, rock, and electronic; that blends composed and improvised music; and that is danceable while still deeply introspective and personal. I also think things like genre and scale—really, any constraint—are optional.
